Smart India Hackathon 2026 · SIH26050 · DRDO · Team leader, GOODFELLASS

Terrain-Aware Counter-UAS System

The problem

At 4,500–5,000 m, ridges block line of sight and fog and snow blind optics. Hardening the hardware can’t fix that, so the system senses at wavelengths terrain and weather don’t block.

Acoustic arraysSignal processingESP32-S3SDRRuggedisationHardware

What we built

  • A full-scale 8-microphone nested acoustic array, designed to hear rotors past a ridge; built and tested at sea level
  • A satellite forward-scatter corridor gate that flags drones crossing mountain passes (designed)
  • Fixes for two altitude errors: a sound-speed bearing bias of up to ~12° and a ×1.29 rotor-tone shift

Drone-Based Landfill Survey System

The problem

Cities pay contractors per tonne to clear legacy dumpsites, but drone surveys give one volume number with no error range, and can’t tell waste that left the site from waste only moved around inside it.

What I’m building

  • Removed tonnage with a 95% confidence interval, from DEM-of-difference change detection and Monte Carlo density modelling
  • Material-zone segmentation that separates real removal from relocation and fresh dumping
  • Thermal hotspot tracking with in-flight alerts from an onboard Jetson
