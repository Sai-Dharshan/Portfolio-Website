High-Altitude Counter-UAS System

The problem

Drones are hard to detect in high-altitude terrain: ridges block line-of-sight, and fog and snow blind cameras.

Sensor fusionRFRadarEO/IRAcousticHardware

What I built

  • Anti-drone detection that fuses RF, radar, EO/IR and acoustic sensors
  • Weather-based sensor weighting that shifts trust between sensors as visibility changes
  • A design aimed at line-of-sight blockage and fog/snow in mountain terrain

Robust Code-Mixed Offensive Content Detection

The problem

Offensive-content classifiers for Tamil-English code-mixed text break when people spell the same word in different transliterations.

What I’m building

  • A transliteration-noise benchmark for character-level robustness
  • Mitigation with data augmentation and consistency training
